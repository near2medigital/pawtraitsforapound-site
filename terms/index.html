1. What you are buying

For £1, you are buying one AI-generated digital pet portrait based on one uploaded photograph of one dog or one cat and one selected launch style. The finished portrait is supplied digitally by email as a JPEG. The launch output is 4:5 and currently generated at 1600 × 2000 pixels.

The total price for one order is £1. Payment is handled securely by SumUp. Pawtraits for a Pound does not receive or store your full card details.

3. Your photograph

You must own the photograph you upload or have permission from the rights holder to use it for this purpose. The photograph should show one dog or cat clearly enough for portrait creation. Please do not upload photographs containing a prominent identifiable person, multiple pets or content you do not have the right to provide.

4. Automated processing and AI-generated artwork

Your photograph is processed using automated systems to check suitability, generate the portrait and quality-check the result. AI-generated artwork involves interpretation. We aim to preserve recognisable identity and important visible markings, but the result will not be a pixel-for-pixel reproduction of the source photograph.

If the uploaded photograph is not suitable for reliable automated portrait creation, we may ask you to provide a clearer photograph of the same pet. A replacement-photo request does not create another charge.
